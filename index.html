Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before you swipe.

Why can’t I see all my photos?

MemoSwipe needs permission to access your photo library. In iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → MemoSwipe, check your access settings. With limited access, only the photos and videos you allow are available; some album features may be unavailable.

You can change which items are shared, or allow full access if you want to organize your entire library. Settings labels may vary by iOS version.

Why does iCloud media take time to load?

If an original photo or video is stored in iCloud and isn’t available on your device, iOS may need to download it first. Loading depends on your connection, iCloud availability, and device storage.

Try a stable internet connection, check available storage, and retry. Media that hasn’t been downloaded may be unavailable offline.

Does swiping left delete the original?

Swiping left only adds an item to the app’s Trash. The original stays in your photo library until you confirm system deletion.

Before system deletion, you can restore items from the app’s Trash. After system deletion, use Recently Deleted in Apple’s Photos app to recover items while they are still available there. Recovery is subject to Apple’s retention rules; permanently deleted items cannot be recovered through MemoSwipe.

With iCloud Photos enabled, system deletion may also sync to your other devices. Review your selection before confirming deletion.

What is the daily free browsing allowance?

Free users can browse 60 items per day, with photos and videos counted together. Members have unlimited browsing.

The allowance resets at the start of each day according to your device’s local calendar. Viewing the same item again on the same day doesn’t count twice. Clearing browsing history doesn’t reset the daily allowance.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the membership purchase screen in MemoSwipe and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the App Store uses the same Apple Account you originally purchased with, connect to the internet, and follow any Apple authentication prompts.

Restoration checks eligible membership purchases with Apple. It does not restore local browsing or organizing history. If your membership still doesn’t appear, email support with the details above; never send passwords or verification codes.

How do I manage or cancel a membership?

Monthly and yearly memberships renew automatically unless canceled. Lifetime membership is a one-time purchase.

To manage or cancel an existing subscription, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MemoSwipe, or use Manage Subscription in the app when available. Check the renewal date and cancellation status shown by Apple.

Buying lifetime membership does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Cancel any subscription you no longer need separately through Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription either.

Where is my browsing and organizing history saved?

Browsing and organizing records are stored locally on your device. Recovery of these records after uninstalling the app is not guaranteed. Resetting browsing history may also remove the corresponding local records.

Restore Purchases restores eligible membership access, not browsing history. Local records are separate from the original photos and videos in your system photo library.
